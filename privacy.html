Privacy Policy

HOSSAM N8N Backup is a personal backup tool operated by Hussam. It is not offered as a public service.

Google Drive access and use

The tool uses Google authorization and rclone to access the owner's authorized Drive account. It uploads encrypted backups, lists files and their metadata, verifies transfers, downloads backups for recovery, and deletes backups selected under the owner's retention settings.

Access is used only for backup and recovery operations. Google account passwords are not collected by the tool.

Storage and sharing

Backup archives are encrypted locally before upload to Google Drive. File names, sizes and other metadata may remain visible to Google. Authorization tokens are stored on the owner's computer for unattended operation.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Backup contents and credentials are not published on this website or in its GitHub repository.

Retention and control

The owner controls backup retention and deletion. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future authorized operations but does not automatically delete existing local or cloud backups.

This website

This informational website has no login form or analytics scripts added by its owner. GitHub Pages hosts the website and may process visitor information under GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

Contact the operator through the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.
